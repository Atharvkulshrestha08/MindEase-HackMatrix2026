# MindEase 2.0: Product Requirements Document

## Product Vision
MindEase is a daily mental wellness ecosystem for students and young professionals. It focuses on AI guidance, personalized activities, habit building, and self-awareness through a playful yet premium experience.

## Target Audience
- Primary: College students (18-26)
- Secondary: Young professionals, remote workers.

## Design Philosophy
- **Vibe:** Playful, calming, premium, modern, emotionally warm.
- **Visuals:** Rounded components (20px), soft shadows, glassmorphism, smooth animations.
- **Brand Colors:** 
  - Primary: Soft Blue (#5DADE2)
  - Secondary: Mint Green (#58D68D)
  - Accent: Warm Yellow (#F4D03F)
  - Warning: Soft Coral (#EC7063)
  - Background: #F8FAFC (Light), #111827 (Dark)

## Key Features & Screen List
1. **Landing Page:** Hero with CTAs, benefits, and feature overview.
2. **Authentication:** Modern login/signup with social options.
3. **Dashboard:** Personalized greeting, mood card, activity widgets, and streaks.
4. **AI Companion (Mind AI):** Conversational interface for emotional guidance and stress management.
5. **Activities Hub:** Breathing, meditation, focus sessions, and music therapy.
6. **Journal:** Rich text editor with AI reflection and emotion detection.
7. **Insights:** Visualized mood trends, stress analysis, and activity reports.
8. **Habit Garden:** Gamified habit tracking with growing plants.
9. **Community:** Anonymous supportive space for sharing wins and challenges.

## Tech & Performance
- Stack: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS.
- Target: Under 2s load time, 95+ Lighthouse score.
